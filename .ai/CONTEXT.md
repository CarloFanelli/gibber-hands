# Glossary

### Drum layers
How many parts of the drum pattern play, from 1 (kick only) to 4 (the full pattern), set by the **Second hand**'s height in **Drum mode**.
_Avoid_: level, volume, intensity (volume is the squeeze's job)

### Drum mode
The **Second hand**'s mode, switched with the rock sign 🤟, in which it plays no note and runs the drum loop: left–right picks the **Drum pattern**, height sets the **Drum layers**, a closed fist mutes.
_Avoid_: beat mode, rhythm mode

### Drum pattern
One of the four fixed drum loops (Rock, House, Hip-hop, Breakbeat), each with its own tempo.
_Avoid_: preset (the synth sound), beat (one count of the bar), groove

### Gesture guide
The list of every gesture and what it does, always in view below the live code, in the same panel. The help sheet opened with "?" stays the full reference, keys included.
_Avoid_: cheat sheet, legend, help (the help sheet)

### Hand size
The distance from the wrist to the middle-finger knuckle. Pinch values are divided by it, so they don't depend on how far the hand is from the camera.
_Avoid_: hand scale, palm length

### Lead hand
The hand that always plays notes: the player's right hand, or the left one after the swap control. It never switches to **Shape mode**.
_Avoid_: main hand, melody hand, playing hand (the **Second hand** plays notes too)

### Note lane
One of the vertical strips that divide the **Playing zone** from left to right: 8, or up to 16 as the camera picture widens. The lane under the **Palm center** picks the note.
_Avoid_: zone (reserved for the playing zone), key, column

### Palm center
The point that moves the note and the brightness: the average of the wrist and the four finger-base knuckles. It stays still while the fingers pinch or change gesture.
_Avoid_: index (the fingertip, which moves when you pinch), fingertip, cursor

### Preset
One of the six named sounds the visitor picks. It bundles a synth character, a scale and an effect.
_Avoid_: patch (Gibber's name for a synth's built-in settings), instrument (the whole app)

### Playing zone
The central 80% of the camera view, horizontally and vertically, where hand movement sets the note and the brightness. Outside it, values stay at the nearest edge.
_Avoid_: control square (handMouse's name for the same idea), active area

### Second hand
The player's other hand. It plays notes like the **Lead hand** until the Victory sign switches it to **Shape mode** or the rock sign 🤟 to **Drum mode**, and back on the same sign again.
_Avoid_: left hand (wrong after a swap), control hand, free hand

### Shape mode
The **Second hand**'s mode in which it plays no note: holding its squeeze raises every note one octave, holding a thumbs-down lowers them one, and its height sets the reverb or echo amount.
_Avoid_: effects mode, control mode

### Step hint
A short prompt over the camera view for the first step the visitor hasn't done yet. It disappears once the step is done.
_Avoid_: tutorial, onboarding card
